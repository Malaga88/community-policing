How GubiWatch works

Three steps. No account required to look.

01

Notice something

A theft, harassment, a broken light on a dark path, or someone who doesn't belong — the small stuff matters as much as the big stuff.

02

Report it your way

Fill a short form with what happened and where. Choose to sign it with your name, or send it anonymously — your call, every time.

03

Campus stays informed

Your report joins the shared dashboard instantly, so every student — not just security — can see what's happening around them.

What you can report

Four categories. Colour-coded on the dashboard.

Theft

Stolen phones, laptops, bikes, or anything taken without consent.

Harassment

Verbal, physical, or persistent unwanted contact of any kind.

Safety hazard

Broken lighting, unsafe structures, blocked exits — anything that could hurt someone.

Suspicious activity

Anyone or anything that feels off, even if you can't say exactly why.
